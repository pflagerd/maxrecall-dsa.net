# "Fundamentals" is a difficulty below Easy, for seven questions; Hello World's topic becomes "Output"

2026-11-08 · decided by the owner · written by questions

**Decided** (owner, 2026-11-08, in the questions session; #93, Part 1 of
`docs/plans/fundamentals-learned-links-concept-checks.md`, #145):

- **One new level, `Fundamentals`**, below Easy: the difficulties become
  `Fundamentals`, `Easy`, `Medium`, `Hard` (Q3; chosen over more than
  one level).
- **Seven questions move to it** (Q1): Hello World, Build a New String,
  Add to a String, Double Each Character, To Uppercase and Lowercase,
  Swap Case with Helpers, and Is Unique I. The proposal had six and kept
  Is Unique I at Easy; the owner added it (chosen over the six, and over
  Hello World alone). The guide's rule is reworded to fit (questions'
  wording, in the plan): Fundamentals when the one new concept is
  something the language or its standard library gives you, learned by
  using it, a ready-made container such as Python's `set` included;
  Easy or above when it's a data structure the learner builds, or an
  algorithm.
- **Hello World's topic `Fundamentals` becomes `Output`** (Q2; chosen
  over keeping it, and over no topic), so the word names only the level.
- **Parts 2 (#88) and 3 (#92) are decided later**, after Part 1 is
  built. Q5 (backlog order) moved to #146 (users).

**Why**: a topic says what a question is about and a difficulty how hard
it is; questions about language features sit below the data-structure
and algorithm questions (owner, #93).

Not decided here: the badge's colour (Q4), which is users' (`style.css`).
Merge order, from the plan: users' `difficulty-fundamentals` rule first,
then questions' change, so the seven badges are never uncoloured.

Source: `docs/plans/fundamentals-learned-links-concept-checks.md`, Part 1;
`question-files.js` (`DIFFICULTIES`).
