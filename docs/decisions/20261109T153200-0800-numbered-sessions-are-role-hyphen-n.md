# Numbered sessions are `<role>-<n>`: "questions-1", not "questions 1"

2026-11-09 (Pacific) · decided by the owner · written by the architect

**Decided** (owner, 2026-11-09, in the architect's session): "I want
questions 1 called questions-1 and questions 2 called questions-2." Then,
asked whether that holds for every role: yes.

- **A numbered session's title** is `maxrecallcode.net:<YYMMDD>:<role>-<n>`,
  for every role (`CLAUDE.md`, "Several sessions of one role"), and it's
  named the same way in writing.
- **Done the same day**: the architect retitled the two sessions,
  `maxrecallcode.net:261109:questions-1` and `…:questions-2`, and told
  both.
- **Older text stays as written**: sessions, handoffs and decision files
  before this say "questions 1", "questions 2".

**Why** (the architect's reading; the owner gave none): one unbroken
token, as role names in titles already are
(`infrastructure-and-security`), so it's searched for as one word.

on this point (it isn't edited).
