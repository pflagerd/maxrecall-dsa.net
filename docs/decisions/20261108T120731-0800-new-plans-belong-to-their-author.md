# A new plan in `docs/plans/` belongs to the role that wrote it, from its first commit

2026-11-08 · decided by the owner · written by the architect

**Decided** (owner, 2026-11-08, in the architect's session): a file a role
adds to `docs/plans/` is that role's from its first commit, as if it
were on its ownership list. The role names itself in the plan's first
lines. The architect adds the file to the role file's Plans list in its
next role-files pull request, as a record (`CLAUDE.md`, "Roles";
`docs/roles/architect.md`, "Each session", step 2). Option A, chosen
over adding each plan by its own role-files pull request, as before (B).

**Why**: three plans started out on no role's list on 2026-11-07 and
2026-11-08: `question-bank-page.md` (users, #133),
`fundamentals-learned-links-concept-checks.md` (questions, #145) and
`quotes-question-and-syntax-checks.md` (questions 2, #156). Each needed
a pull request of the architect's, and each author's edits until then
were to a file it didn't own.

Source: `CLAUDE.md`, "Roles"; `docs/roles/architect.md`, "Each session".
