# Sessions are titled `maxrecallcode.net:<YYMMDD>:<role>`, and the architect's poller finds them so

2026-11-08 · decided by the owner · written by the architect

**Decided** (owner, 2026-11-08, in the architect's session): every
session's title is `maxrecallcode.net:<YYMMDD>:<role>`, and `CLAUDE.md`,
"Updates while idle", finds the session of the role concerned by that
title, the newest one. Sessions from before 2026-11-08 keep
`maxrecall-dsa.net:<YYMMDD>:<role>`; the poller only needs the newest.

**Why**: on 2026-11-08 the architect's and the VPS operator's sessions
were titled `maxrecallcode.net:261108:<role>`, while the rule named only
`maxrecall-dsa.net:<YYMMDD>:<role>`. A role whose session had the new
prefix would have gone unmessaged. The owner first chose to accept either
prefix, so nothing had to be renamed; then renamed that day's users,
questions and infrastructure and security sessions to the new prefix, and
chose the one prefix instead (over "either", and over any prefix, which
could match another project's session).

Source: `CLAUDE.md`, "Updates while idle"; session titles from
`list_sessions`, 2026-11-08 16:53 and 16:57 UTC.
